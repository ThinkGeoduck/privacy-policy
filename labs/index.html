Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

The short version: this site has no accounts, no cookies, no ads, no analytics and no trackers. Files you give the labs stay in your browser.

heyutkarsh.me is an independent research and side projects site run by Utkarsh Gupta. This policy covers the front page and the labs hosted on it, currently the Genetic Music Lab and the Genetic Maze.

What we collect

Nothing. The site does not ask for your name, email or any other personal information, and there is nothing to sign up for.

  • No cookies are set.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking scripts are loaded.
  • Nothing is saved in your browser's storage.
  • Fonts, sounds and code are served from this site itself, not from third-party services.

MIDI files in the Genetic Music Lab

When you load a MIDI file into the Genetic Music Lab, it is read and processed entirely inside your browser. The file is never uploaded to a server, and it is gone when you close or reload the page.

The lab plays sound through your browser's audio system. It never uses your microphone or records anything.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel's servers receive basic technical information whenever a page is requested, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request. Vercel uses this to deliver the site and to protect it from abuse, and handles it under its own privacy policy. We do not use this information to identify or profile visitors.

Children's privacy

The site is suitable for general audiences, including students. Because it collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect any from children.
