Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026

Thank you for using Is It Raining?. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information We Collect

Location

Is It Raining? requests your device's location to determine whether it is currently raining in your area.

Your location is used only to provide weather information and is not stored by us.

Advertising

This app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may automatically collect certain information such as:

  • Device information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Approximate location
  • Usage data

This information is collected and processed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

Google Privacy Policy

Weather Data

Is It Raining? retrieves weather information based on your location to determine whether it is raining. Weather data is requested only when needed and is not stored by us.

Third-Party Services

This application may use third-party services, including:

  • Google AdMob
  • Your weather data provider (e.g. OpenWeather, Open-Meteo, WeatherAPI)

These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Is It Raining? is suitable for general audiences. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
